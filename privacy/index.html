Rainsong Privacy Policy

Rainsong: Mahjong Solitaire, by Dizzywalk Records LLC. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Rainsong is a calm tile-matching game. Solo play needs no account. With friends is optional: it creates a private player account with a display name you choose. We do not ask for your email, phone number or address book.

What stays on your device

Your solo progress, scrolls, settings, kept cards and letters are saved on your phone, tablet or in your browser. Solo saves are not uploaded to our Friends service. Deleting the app or clearing its data may remove these local saves; your device's backup settings may preserve a copy. Shared paintings use the separate service described below.

With friends

When you choose With friends, our service stores your display name, a random player ID, your accepted friends, invitations and shared painting progress. It also stores any short note or reaction you choose to send with a section. Your friends in that painting can see its progress and notes. There is no public player directory. Adding a friend requires an invitation and acceptance.

The service is hosted by Render in the United States. Requests are encrypted in transit. We store a hash of your private access key, not the key itself. The app stores the key on your device, separately from ordinary save exports and analytics. A recovery key lets you deliberately restore access on another device; keep it private. Deleting the app does not delete your Friends account.

If you block a player, the service stores that choice to prevent further contact. If you report a player, it stores your selected reason and relevant shared content for moderation for up to 90 days, including after account deletion. Reports are visible to the game's operator, not other players. The community rules explain these controls.

Move logs are checked to validate a completed section and are not saved on the server. Retry records contain request hashes. Our application does not log names, notes, invitation codes or access keys. Hosting infrastructure may retain connection metadata and backup snapshots under its own retention schedule. Friends data is used to operate this feature, not sent to our advertising or analytics partners.

To delete your Friends account and associated personal data, open With friends, Friends account, Delete Friends account, or use our account deletion page. Deletion removes your profile, access key, friendships, invitations and notes from the active service. Your name is replaced with “Former painter” in shared paintings. Other painters keep the already revealed artwork and anonymous section progress; unfinished shared paintings close. Your local solo progress is separate. Backup copies expire under the hosting provider's retention schedule.

Letters

When you send a letter, your note is packed inside the link itself, after the "#" in the address. That part of a link is not sent to our web server when your friend opens it. Only the people you share the link with can read the note. We never see or store it.

Play statistics

To make the game better, the app counts simple play events, such as a board started, a board finished, a hint used, an ad watched or a setting changed. Each event carries an anonymous random install id created by the app, the app version, the platform (iOS, Android or web), a session id, game details such as the board played, and, when you installed from an ad, the ad campaign that brought you. These events are sent to PostHog, our analytics provider, which processes them for us.

  • They never include your notes, names, contacts, photos or anything you type.
  • We do not sell them to data brokers.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, a report goes to Sentry, our crash reporting provider. It holds the error, the app version, the device model and operating system, and the same anonymous install id. It never holds your notes or anything you type.

Ads

Ads keep Rainsong free. In the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, ads are served through AppLovin MAX, which may pass the request to its partner ad networks. To choose and measure ads, AppLovin and those networks may receive your device's advertising identifier, its IP address, the device model and similar technical details, as your phone's settings allow. Ads never appear in the middle of a board. Settings, Report an ad lets you tell us about an ad that seems wrong.

Measuring our own ads

We advertise Rainsong in other apps and on social networks. To learn which of our ads bring players who enjoy the game, the app tells these measurement partners that it was installed or opened, about ads watched and purchases made in the game, and about a few milestones, such as finishing 10 boards or playing on a second day:

  • Tenjin, our attribution provider, which matches an install to the ad that led to it. On Android it reads the Google Play install referrer; on iPhone it uses Apple's privacy-preserving ad attribution (SKAdNetwork / AdAttributionKit).
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads), through the Meta SDK's app events.
  • Google (Google Ads), through Google Analytics for Firebase.

With these events go the advertising identifier (where allowed), an app-set or install id, the IP address and basic device details. The partners use them to measure and improve our ad campaigns. Your notes and letters are never sent.

Your ad choices

  • On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is shared only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google, Ads, or Privacy, Ads).
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first. Without it you still see ads, but they are not personalized, and our measurement partners receive only what consent allows. You can change your answer any time under Settings, Privacy choices.

Purchases

Rainsong may offer optional in-app purchases. They are paid through Apple's App Store or Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The store tells us which item you bought, its price and a transaction id so we can deliver it, and we record the purchase (item, price, currency) in our play statistics and with Tenjin, Meta and Google so we can measure our ads. A purchase provider such as RevenueCat may process the receipt for us to confirm it is genuine.

Notifications

If you allow it, Rainsong schedules a gentle daily reminder on your device. These notifications are set up locally on your phone; no notification account or token is created on our side.

Sharing and saving

When you share or save a card, the app hands the image to your device's share sheet or photo library, and only when you ask. We do not read your photo library.

Children

Rainsong is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can clear local saves through your device or browser settings. To remove an online Friends account, use Delete Friends account; uninstalling alone does not delete it.
  • You can ask us to delete the play statistics and crash reports tied to your install id by writing to us. Include the approximate date you installed the game so we can find it. We will also ask our measurement partners to delete the data they hold for your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
