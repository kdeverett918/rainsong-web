Friends community rules

Effective 16 October 2026 · Dizzywalk Records LLC

With friends is a shared space for finishing paintings with people you choose. These rules apply to your display name, invitations, section notes and reactions.

  • Be kind. Do not harass, threaten, impersonate or target someone with hateful language.
  • Do not share sexual content, exploitation, illegal material, scams or unwanted advertising.
  • Keep private information private. Do not post someone else's contact details or personal information without their permission.
  • Invite people who want to play with you. Respect a person's decision to leave or block you.

Block or report

Use a friend's options in With friends to report a name or note, or block that person. Blocking removes the friendship, closes unfinished paintings you share and prevents new shared paintings together. Reporting sends the selected reason and relevant shared content to Rainsong for review.

We may remove content or restrict an account that breaks these rules. Reports are reviewed by the game's operator. To contact us about a report, email kristine@dizzywalk.com. Do not send your private recovery key.

Your choices

You can play solo without a Friends account. Read our privacy policy or delete your Friends account.
