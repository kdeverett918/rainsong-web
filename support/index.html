Rainsong Support

Rainsong: Mahjong Solitaire, by Dizzywalk Records LLC.

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Write to kristine@dizzywalk.com. Please include your device and the app version (Settings, then About).

How do I play?

Tap two matching tiles that are free. A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and at least its left or right side is open. Clear every pair to finish the board and its painting.

A tile will not select

It is locked. The game shows which tiles block it and why ("Covered by a tile on top" or "Locked by left and right"). Clear those first.

I am stuck

Use Undo, Hint or Shuffle. They are free, and there is no timer. Every board can be solved.

Tiles or text are too small

Open Settings and turn on Large tiles, or raise Text size up to 140%.

Letters

Send a letter from the finish card or the Letters tab. Your friend opens the link and plays the board in their browser, no download needed. Your note is inside the link; we never see it.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
