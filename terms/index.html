The game

Rainsong is free to play. It shows ads and offers an optional in-app purchase. Your progress is saved on your device.

No ads

  • No ads is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Its price is shown in the app in your local currency before you buy, and is charged by the App Store or Google Play.
  • It removes interstitial and banner ads for good. Optional rewarded videos (for a helper, double Raindrops or a revive) stay available; you choose each one.
  • Use Restore purchases (Settings) to bring No ads back on a new device or after a reinstall.
  • Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play under their policies. A refunded purchase ends its benefit, and ads return.
